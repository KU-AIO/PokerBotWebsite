Build a bot that plays poker on its own.

  • When Saturday, April 3, 2027
  • Who Any student, any school, any experience level
  • Sign up Opens January 2027 (planned)

♠What it is

You write a program that plays poker by itself. It looks at its cards and the table, then decides when to bet, call or fold, with no one pressing the buttons for it. Then the bots play each other.

You don't need any poker or AI background. If you're curious about building a bot that makes its own decisions, you can take part.

♥How it works (planned)

The plan is for teams to build and test their bots during a submission window lasting a few weeks. The competition then wraps up in person on event day.

♦Timeline

  1. January 2027 (planned) Registration opens.
  2. Early March 2027 (planned) The submission window opens. Exact dates will be announced.
  3. Saturday, April 3, 2027 Event day, in person.
