FAQ

Why poker?

Poker, as a game of making decisions under uncertainty, is a great application of understanding and applying game theory, probability, decision-making skills, and your intuition. We believe taking this challenging game to the next step by having students apply their knowledge through code is a way to showcase unique problem-solving and creativity through a competition.

How does it work?

Once the submission window opens, build and test a poker-playing bot in Python within our guidelines. Your final submission goes up against every other competitor's bot, and the results wrap up in person on event day.

Who can join?

Any student from any school, at any experience level.

Do I need poker or coding experience?

No. Beginners are welcome.

Which programming language?

Python.
